What Tempest-ARKNET solves

  • The deterministic blind spot: Running a 6-DOF Monte Carlo on one forecast hides wind shear, gusts, and air-density swings, creating deceptively narrow landing zones.
  • Calibrated atmospheric ensembles: Tempest-ARKNET generates 199 coherent vertical profiles across 44 heights (10 m to 18 km AGL), spanning wind, temperature, moisture, gusts, and vertical motion.
  • Forecast-to-pad coupling: A single-step Engression generator pairs with IGRAv2 balloon calibration, Schaake Shuffle shear restoration, and live launch-day sensor assimilation for 6-DOF flight sims.

Single Deterministic Forecast vs. Calibrated 199-Member Flight Envelope

How full-column wind shear uncertainty translates directly into honest 6-DOF apogee and landing bounds
Why ensembles matter for rockets: 87% of wind-driven apogee spread originates in the $0\text{–}300\text{ m}$ launch-rail layer, while mid-altitude shear ($5\text{–}12\text{ km}$) governs drift and structural load. Tempest-ARKNET propagates $M = 199$ vertically coherent profiles rather than a single mean line.
