Flight-Sim & Monte Carlo

Sub-microsecond 10 m lookup tables with continuous 0 m and 18 km boundaries, MIL-HDBK-1797 Dryden turbulence, and joint drag-vs-drift variance attribution.

6.1

Profile readers & boundary defaults

Querying non-uniform 44-level altitude grids at 100–500 Hz quickly bottlenecks a 6-DOF solver. Tempest-ARKNET compiles each ensemble member once into a uniform 10 m float32 lookup table with continuous boundaries below 10 m and above 18 km.

  • air="model" (default thermodynamic source): Uses Tempest-ARKNET’s generated temperature, pressure, density, and speed of sound profiles directly rather than substituting a dry 1976 ISA atmosphere (air="isa" remains available for wind-only sensitivity studies).
  • above_top="hold" above 18 km AGL:
    • Continuous stratospheric extension: Holds the 18 km horizontal wind vector constant while integrating $(T, \ln p)$ smoothly up to 86 km through the continuous ISA stratospheric tail.
    • Alias and alternative modes: "climatology" aliases "hold" to prevent any step cliff at 18 km; opt-in alternatives are "decay" ($e^{-\Delta z / 5000\text{ m}}$) and "raise" (throws AboveTopError).
  • below_10m="linear" (default pad-to-10 m bridge): Interpolates linearly between the 0 m pad node and the 10 m AGL level so rail-exit winds stay smooth without terrain metadata; "loglaw" is available when AERSURFACE roughness $z_0$ is supplied.

6.2

Dryden turbulence & variance attribution

Synoptic 44-level profiles capture mesoscale wind shear, not sub-100 m eddies near the rail or Max-Q. Tempest-ARKNET overlays MIL-HDBK-1797 Dryden turbulence and separates atmospheric dispersion from vehicle uncertainty in joint Monte Carlo runs.

Euler sub-stepping vs. exact discrete AR(2)

Mode Discretization Method Behavior & Trade-Off
exact_ar=False (default) Exact AR(1) for vertical $w$; explicit Euler sub-stepping (nsub = 8 sub-steps per $\Delta z$) with burn-in warmup for transverse $(u, v)$. Supports height-varying length scales $L_u(z), L_w(z)$ with simple explicit updates, but needs pre-$z=0$ burn-in and adds small discretization error when $\Delta z / L_u$ is large.
exact_ar=True (opt-in S1) Exact discrete spatial $\Delta z$ AR(2) state transition for the second-order transverse $(u, v)$ Dryden filter with stationary initialization. Eliminates sub-stepping and burn-in warmup while matching analytical MIL-HDBK-1797 variance and autocorrelation across arbitrary vertical grid steps $\Delta z$.

Separating drag vs. drift variance

Raw ensemble indices ($0, 1, \dots, N-1$) are arbitrary and meaningless in regression. Instead, atmosphere_metrics() summarizes each member into three physical scalars so rank regression and Sobol indices separate aerodynamic drag variance from wind-drift variance:

  • pad_air_density ($\rho_{\text{pad}}$ at $z_0 + 1\text{ m}$): Captures launch-site air density variations driven by temperature, pressure, and humidity—the primary atmospheric driver of aerodynamic drag, Max-Q, and apogee altitude dispersion.
  • mean_wind_0_200m (low-level rail & weathercocking shear): Averages horizontal wind across the first 200 m above the pad where rocket velocity is lowest and aerodynamic weathercocking torque sets the initial flight-path tilt.
  • mean_wind_0_3km (ascent & descent drift column): Captures bulk boundary-layer and lower-tropospheric transport responsible for horizontal downrange drift during boost and main-chute descent.
  • Incremental atmospheric variance gain: explained() fits joint OLS rank regression to report r2_vehicle, r2_all, and atmosphere_gain = max(r2_all - r2_vehicle, 0), showing immediately whether tightening weather forecasts or tightening motor/mass tolerances yields the larger safety payoff.
