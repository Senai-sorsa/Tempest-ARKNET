How to Read These Benchmarks (Plain English)

Every experiment in this ledger is graded on three concrete questions. CRPS skill measures how much sharper and closer to reality our generated skies are than guessing the 15-year seasonal average (higher is better). 99% coverage (cov99) checks whether real weather actually lands inside our outer safety bounds 99% of the time—like testing a range safety net. Seed noise is the tiny random wobble from re-training with different random numbers; any improvement smaller than that wobble counts as a tie, not a real win.

Dual Statistical Gate

Every change had to pass both compare_headline.py (skill15 & cov99) and compare_shape.py (8 shape metrics: PIT, coverage, skew, kurtosis, tails, events, vertical corr, shear KS) with zero WORSE flags.

Seed-Noise Yardstick (IL-92)

Measured across 3 seeds of p2f: ERA5 skill15 spread 0.0006, balloons 0.0013, |cov99-0.99| 0.0004 / 0.0003. Audited with fair CRPS (AU-1) and 21-day bootstrap blocks (AU-4, 1.60× wider CIs).

2024–25 Dev vs. 2026 Holdout

Train 1991–2020, Calibrate 2021–23, Dev 2024–25. Single-use untouched 2026 Jan–Jun final test: ERA5 +0.0209, Balloons +0.0187, beats Gaussian proxy by +5.6% / +2.2%.

Flight-Sim Reality Check

On 3 Spaceport America flights, vehicle errors (+5–8%) are 2–4× larger than weather effects. 87% of wind apogee spread comes from 0–300 m. Hybrid air="standard" + Tempest wind achieved lowest MAE (4.89%).

5 Transferable Probabilistic-ML & Physics Lessons

Distilled from all 60 logged trials across 1991–2026:

01 · SCORING RULES

Proper Scoring Rules > Heavier Backbones

Targeted proper-scoring losses (afCRPS + shear_tail + c4w) consistently beat heavier Transformers (TokenGenerator), Rectified Flows (GF3), and 8-seed ensembles (IL-3).

02 · COORDINATES

Fix Coordinate Space Before Hacking Loss

Moving humidity from bounded [0, 100]% RH to smooth log dewpoint depression (dpd) cut lower-tail misses 4.7× without brittle boundary penalties.

03 · SAMPLE SIZE

Respect Effective Sample Size

Serial weather correlation makes naive intervals overconfident. 21-day block bootstrap (AU-4, 1.60× wider CIs) and the IL-92 seed-noise yardstick stop false wins.

04 · BIAS VS. SPREAD

Don’t Fix Mean Bias with Spread Inflation

Stretching ensemble spread (AV11, UR1, G2-2) degrades CRPS when the true defect is a local mean offset—fix bias directly via IL-98B EWMA or IL-207b site factors.

05 · CLEAN WORLDS

Keep Input Worlds & Benchmarks Clean

Don’t merge optional inputs into one masked trunk (N4), don’t refit on short shifted windows (UR11), and never judge atmosphere-only dispersion against rocket vehicle errors.
